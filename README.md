## 🌻 Tips for Your First Garden

- Start with easy plants like **lettuce**, **radishes**, or **herbs** (basil, mint)–they grow fast and forgiving
- Water deeply but less often–this helps roots grow strong
- Place your pots where they get at least 6 hours of sunlight a day–most veggies love sun
- Use good soil from a garden store–extra nutrients make a big difference
- Be patient–seeds take time, but watching them sprout is so rewarding
